How to detect a gas leak
  • Smell for a strong gas odour near the cylinder or stove
  • Listen for hissing sounds around valves or connections
  • Apply soapy water to connections and check for bubbles
  • If unsure, turn off the gas and contact us immediately

If you notice a strong gas smell, ventilate the area and contact us for assistance.
